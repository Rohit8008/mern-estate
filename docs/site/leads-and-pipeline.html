Leads and pipeline

How leads, follow-ups, scoring, sequences, deals, buyers, owners, tasks and the calendar work together for an agency team.

Who is who

Three kinds of people live in three different screens. Mixing them up is the most common mistake.

ConceptScreen (default label)RouteWhat it is
Lead / clientClients/clients, /clients/:idA sales lead: someone who may buy or rent. The Client model; each has a status, priority, source, budget, score, deals, follow-ups and a communications log. A "contact type" marks it as lead, co_agent or referral_partner.
OwnerProperty Owners/ownersA landlord or seller whose property you list. Name, contact details, company, address, optional tax id (hidden from normal reads), active flag. Not a sales lead.

Who sees which leads

  • An admin sees every lead in the workspace and may filter by agent.
  • An employee sees and edits only leads assigned to them, whatever the query string says. This holds for the list, export, bulk actions, pipeline, bottlenecks, follow-ups, ROI report and sequences.
  • On top of that, the Clients endpoints need a permission: viewClients, createClient, updateClient, deleteClient, exportData. Admins pass every permission check; an employee needs a custom role that grants them (an employee with no role gets "No role assigned"). See Team, roles and permissions.
  • The CRM endpoints (/api/crm/*: deals, follow-ups, communications, summary, pipeline) check only that the caller is an admin or employee and, for single-lead calls, that the lead is theirs. They do not check a permission key.

Create and find leads

To add a lead: Clients, then New client (the command palette's "New client" opens the same form via /clients?new=1). Requires createClient. Only the name is required by the model.

  • Duplicates are refused. The server looks across the whole workspace (not just your leads) for the same email, or for the same phone using the last ten digits (so "+91 98765 43210" and "09876543210" match). A hit answers 409 with the existing lead's name, owner and status. The Clients form shows the existing lead and a Create anyway button, which resends with ?force=true (the same query the API accepts).
  • Who owns it. If you name no owner, the workspace's lead-assignment rule picks one (see Assignment). An employee cannot assign a lead to anyone but themselves.
  • The score is calculated at creation, so a new lead with a budget and an urgent priority does not start cold.

The list has three views: Cards, Board and Table. Filters: search text, status, contact type, owner (admin), tag and temperature. The table sorts and pages on the server (sortable: name, score, temperature, status, priority, budget, next follow-up, email, created, updated). Export downloads a CSV of everything matching the current filters (not just the page; capped at 50,000 rows) and needs exportData.

Bulk actions

Tick rows, then use the bar: assign (admin only), change status, delete, and tag or untag (API actions assign, status, delete, tag, untag). Up to 500 ids at once. Requires updateClient. An employee's selection is narrowed to the leads they own before anything is written; ids they do not own are silently skipped. Deleting a lead is a soft delete.

Lead score and temperature

client.calculateScore() is the only place the score is computed. It adds five factors and caps the total at 100:

FactorPoints
Engagement10 per communication logged in the last 30 days, max 30
Budget20 if a maximum budget is set, otherwise 0
Urgencyby priority: low 5, medium 10, high 20, urgent 30
Fit5 per interested listing, max 20
Recencyby days since last contact (or last update): 7 or fewer 20; up to 30 days 12; up to 90 days 5; beyond that 0

Temperature follows the score: 60 and above is hot, 30 and above is warm, below that cold.

The score is recomputed when a lead is created, when it is edited, when a communication is logged, edited or deleted, when a deal changes stage, and nightly at 03:00 (UTC, job lead-rescore), because the recency factor decays with time.

Manual temperature is pinned. If you choose Hot, Warm or Cold on the lead's Overview (the Temperature control says "Set by hand — the nightly score will not change it"), the server sets temperatureManual and stops deriving temperature from the score. The score itself keeps updating. Choosing Auto on the Temperature control (or sending temperature: "auto" in a client update) clears temperatureManual and lets the score decide again.

Won and lost leads are never rescored by the nightly job, so past reports do not change. (The job pages through every open lead, 1,000 at a time, so large workspaces are refreshed in full, and it does not bump updatedAt.)

Working a lead

Open a lead from Clients (/clients/:id). The header shows Active Deals, Won Value, Pending Follow-ups and Lead Score. Tabs: Overview, Deals, Follow-ups, Timeline, Calls/Notes, Tasks, Documents.

Overview

  • Contact information, source, requirements (budget range, property type, preferred locations, free text) with inline edit, and tags (see Tags).
  • Temperature control (above).
  • Assigned to and, for an admin, a "Reassign to" picker listing active admins and employees. Reassigning asks for confirmation when the lead already has an owner. The API route is POST /api/clients/:id/assign; it requires the caller to be an admin and to hold updateUser (admins always pass). The new owner gets a notification.
  • Interested in: search listings and link them to the lead (POST /api/clients/:id/interested/add and /remove, needs updateClient). Each linked listing adds 5 to the score's "fit" factor, up to 20.
  • Follow-up sequences the lead is enrolled in, and an email opt-out control (see Sequences).
  • Photos: attach images to the lead (uploaded to Cloudinary from the browser; only the URL is stored).

Calls and notes (communications)

Log Activity records one of call, email, sms, meeting, whatsapp, site_visit, note, a direction (outbound by default or inbound), a summary (2 to 500 characters), optional details, duration in minutes and an outcome. Logging sets the lead's "last contact" time and recalculates the score.

Each entry has Edit and Delete icons. Only the person who logged the entry, or an admin, may edit or delete it; anyone else gets 403 "You can only edit a communication you logged". Deleting moves "last contact" back to the newest remaining entry and rescoring follows, so a mistaken entry no longer skews the number for good.

Follow-ups

To schedule one: lead, Follow-ups tab, set a due date and time, a type (call, email, meeting, site_visit, whatsapp, other) and notes. Mark it Done to complete it. The lead's "next follow-up" is always the earliest pending one. The followup-reminders job (every five minutes) sends the owner a reminder once a follow-up is due, once per follow-up.

Follow-ups due widget. The Dashboard (/dashboard) has a "Follow-ups due" card with Leads and Buyers tabs, showing the next 7 days with Overdue and Today badges. It reads GET /api/crm/follow-ups/upcoming?days=7 (an employee gets their own leads) and /api/dashboard/buyer-stats.

Deals, stages and commission

A lead can hold several deals. To add one: lead, Deals tab, Add deal (stage, value, commission percentage, notes). You can also add a quick deal from the pipeline board. Each deal keeps a stageHistory of who moved it and when.

The stages are a fixed vocabulary in backend/tenancy/stageCatalogue.js; a workspace chooses which to use, their order, names and colours (Settings, Sales pipeline). The default set is every non-legacy stage:

KeyDefault labelNotes
new_leadNew Leaddefault for a new deal
contactedContacted
qualifiedQualified
site_visit_scheduledSite Visit
negotiationNegotiation
booking_tokenBooking / Token
documentationDocumentation
closed_wonWonend state, required in every pipeline
closed_lostLostend state, required in every pipeline
initial_contact, site_visit_done, payment_pendingInitial Contact, Site Visit Done, Payment Pendinglegacy; a workspace can still enable them, but the deal-write validation accepts only the nine non-legacy keys

What happens when a deal is marked Won (PATCH /api/crm/:id/deals/:dealId/stage):

  • the lead's status becomes won and convertedAt is set;
  • every active sequence enrollment for that lead stops immediately;
  • if the deal has no linked transaction, a completed Transaction is created automatically (amount, commission, type, agent), and the linked listing's status becomes sold (or rented for a rent or lease deal). If saving fails afterwards the transaction is soft-deleted and the listing status restored;
  • the score is recalculated.

Lost: status becomes lost, lostAt is set, lostReason is the notes you gave (or "Deal lost"), and sequences stop. A workspace rule can veto a stage change before anything is written (for example "ask for a reason before marking a deal lost"); the user sees the rule's message as a 400. See Rules.

Commission. A deal carries a commission percentage (0 to 100), an amount (value times percentage) and a status: pending, partial or paid. Edit percentage and status from the deal on the lead (PATCH /api/crm/:id/deals/:dealId/commission). The amount is recomputed from the deal's current value whenever the percentage changes.

The deal endpoints (add a deal, change stage) validate against the workspace's enabled stages, not just the catalogue, and answer 400 for a stage the workspace has turned off. A deal already sitting on a now-disabled stage can still be saved at that stage.

Sales pipeline board

Sales Pipeline (/pipeline, requires viewClients to appear in the sidebar) shows one column per stage in the workspace's configured order and labels. Drag a deal card to another column to move it, which calls the same stage endpoint (so the Won and Lost effects above apply). The column totals are the number of deals and their summed value. Leads that have no deal yet appear in the column matching their status (lead maps to New Lead, proposal and negotiation to Negotiation, won to Won, lost to Lost) and can be given a deal quickly with a value. A deals export (CSV, one row per deal) is available at GET /api/crm/pipeline/export.

Bottlenecks

A card above the board shows the average days deals spend in each stage (from stage history), the slowest stage that has at least three samples, and open deals idle for 14 days or more (up to 50 listed). Data comes from GET /api/crm/pipeline/bottlenecks; employees see only their own deals.

Assignment rules

The workspace setting workflow.leadAssignment decides who owns a new lead that arrives without a named owner. It is stored in the workspace's workflow config and edited through PATCH /api/tenant/config (admin only); Settings, Menu & screens has a Lead assignment select (Manual, Round robin, By locality) that saves it; a new workspace stays on manual until an admin changes it.

ModeBehaviour
manual (default)The creator owns it.
round_robinGoes to the active admin or employee holding the fewest open (not won or lost) leads; ties broken by a stable id order.
by_localityGoes to whoever already owns the most leads in the lead's first preferred location (case-insensitive exact match); falls back to the least-loaded agent for a new locality.

An explicitly named owner always wins over the rule. An automatically assigned owner other than the creator is notified (lead.assigned). The lead importer applies the same rule in bulk, see Sharing and imports.

Tags

Tags are a workspace taxonomy: a named, coloured row referenced by id, so renaming or recolouring applies everywhere. Colours: slate, red, amber, emerald, sky, indigo, violet, pink, teal. Names are unique per workspace case-insensitively ("Hot Lead" equals "hot lead"). Leads and listings can be tagged (POST /api/tags/:kind/:id/:tagId, kind client or listing); an employee can tag only records they own. Anyone signed in can create a tag; renaming and deleting are admin-only (Settings, Tags). The older free-text tags array stays on records that already have values.

Lead sources, cost and ROI

The Source on a lead is chosen from the workspace's lead sources. The first read in a workspace seeds the defaults: Walk-in, Referral, Website, Phone enquiry, WhatsApp, Property portal, Social media, Print advertising, Hoarding, Repeat client, Other. An admin manages them in Settings, Lead sources: name, a monthly cost, description and active flag. Deleting a source that leads still use answers 409 with a count.

The ROI report (GET /api/lead-sources/roi, needs viewAnalytics; optional startDate and endDate, default the last 30 days) groups leads created in the period by source and returns, per source: leads, won, lost, revenue (summed value of deals in closed_won), cost (monthly cost times the period in 30-day months), conversion rate, cost per lead, cost per deal and ROI percent. If no cost is on file, cost per lead, cost per deal and ROI are null ("unknown"), not zero. Employees see only their own leads in the report.

Follow-up sequences

A sequence is an ordered list of steps, each with a delay in days (0 to 365) after the previous step, and an action:

  • task: create a task for the lead's owner, due now;
  • email: send the lead an email (subject and body support merge fields written {{firstName}}: recipientName, firstName, lastName, phone, email, workspaceName, date);
  • reminder: notify the owner to make contact.

Admins create, edit and delete sequences in Settings, Follow-up sequences (requireAdmin); any admin or employee can list them. To enroll a lead: open the lead, Follow-up sequences card, enroll. An employee can enroll only their own leads. Enrolling again restarts the sequence. The runner (sequence-steps job, every 15 minutes) behaves like this:

  • a step fires exactly once because the enrollment advances in the same pass;
  • an enrollment stops the moment its lead is won, lost or deleted, or its sequence is paused or deleted. Marking a deal won or lost stops it synchronously, so no further chase email goes out;
  • you cannot enroll a lead that is already won or lost (400);
  • a failing step still advances, with the failure recorded in the enrollment history, so one bad address cannot freeze it;
  • an email step is skipped when the lead has no email or the address is on the workspace's suppression list. Every sequence email carries a footer and the List-Unsubscribe headers. Task and reminder steps still run for unsubscribed leads because they go to the agent.

The lead's email opt-out is shown on the Overview. An agent can record an opt-out on the lead's behalf; undoing it is admin-only, and an opt-out the person made themselves (via the link) cannot be undone by staff (409).

Buyer requirements and matching

Buyer Requirements (/buyers) records what a buyer wants: name, contact, sale or rent, property category (residential, commercial, land, any), min and max price, minimum bedrooms and bathrooms, preferred location, priority (low, medium, high), a follow-up date and a status: active, matched, closed, inactive. Setting the status to matched also stamps the requirement's last-contact date. Admins and employees see every requirement in the workspace; other roles see only those they created.

To see matches: open a requirement's matches (GET /api/buyer-requirements/:id/matches). The server searches only listings you are allowed to see (admin: all; employee: own, assigned or in their assigned categories) with the hard criteria applied in the database, takes up to 200, and ranks them with a 0 to 100 matching score: property type 30 points, price range 20 (half for a one-sided fit), bedrooms 15, bathrooms 15, location substring 20, normalised over the checks the buyer actually set. A match can be saved onto the requirement and removed again.

Requirements with a follow-up date show up under the Buyers tab of the Dashboard's "Follow-ups due" card.

Property owners

Property Owners (/owners) is a plain address book for the people behind your stock. Permissions: viewOwners, createOwner, updateOwner, deleteOwner. Email is unique per workspace when present. The list pages and sorts on the server when page is passed; without it, /api/owner/list returns a bare array (the mobile app and the listing form rely on that). Deleting is soft. A listing can reference several owners.

Tasks and calendar

Tasks (/tasks) are to-dos with status todo, in_progress, review, done, blocked (default todo), priority low, medium (default), high, urgent, a due date, reminders and an optional link to a lead, listing or transaction. Everyone sees their own tasks; an admin may filter by assignee and assign to others. Creating a task for a lead requires that lead to be yours (admin: any). A non-admin cannot reassign a task. The task-reminders job sends reminders every five minutes. The tasks API is gated by viewClients for every action, including create and delete.

Calendar (/calendar) overlays your tasks (by due date), your follow-ups (/api/crm/follow-ups/range) and personal events (title, date, time, reminder minutes before; stored per user on the server, not in the browser). Event times are wall-clock strings, so they do not shift between timezones.

Team performance (admin)

The Dashboard shows a Team performance card to admins only (GET /api/dashboard/employee-performance, admin-only): per employee, assigned listings, sold listings, assigned buyers, closed buyers and a conversion rate.
